About the Truck

Krave Kulture is a Haitian, Caribbean and soul food truck serving Miami, FL. Every complete comes with rice, meat, plantain and mac, cooked to order at the window. The truck has no fixed address: it moves around the city, and today's spot is always posted on Instagram @eatkravekulture.

Frequently Asked Questions

What comes with a complete?
Every complete comes with rice, meat, plantain and mac.
What wing flavors do you have?
Honey hot, lemon pepper, BBQ or buffalo, on 6, 8 or 10 piece baskets with fries included.
