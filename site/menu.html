    About the Truck

    Krave Kulture is a Haitian, Caribbean and soul food truck serving Miami, FL. Every dinner comes with rice, meat and plantain, cooked to order at the window. The truck has no fixed address: it moves around the city, and today's spot is always posted on Instagram @eatkravekulture.

    Catering & Events

    Bring the truck to your birthday, wedding, church event, work lunch or block party. Tell us the date, place and how many people, and we'll get back to you with menu options and pricing.

    What comes with a dinner?
    Every dinner comes with rice, meat and plantain.
    What wing flavors do you have?
