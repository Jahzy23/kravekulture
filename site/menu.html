About the Truck

Krave Kulture is a Haitian, Caribbean and soul food truck serving Miami, FL. Every dinner comes with rice, meat, plantain and mac, cooked to order at the window. The truck has no fixed address: it moves around the city, and today's spot is always posted on Instagram @eatkravekulture.

Frequently Asked Questions

What comes with a dinner?
Every dinner comes with rice, meat, plantain and mac.
What wing flavors do you have?
Honey hot, lemon pepper, BBQ or buffalo, on 6, 8 or 10 piece baskets with fries included.
Where do I find the truck today?
The truck moves. Today's spot is always posted on Instagram @eatkravekulture.
How much are the dinners?
$20 to $25, depending on the meat. Every dinner comes with rice, meat, plantain and mac.
